## What it shows

- **At the ceiling in both arms, as for the first three skills**: the frames process and `block_eq`
  carry the MCP runs to 44, and the trap the test was built around (drain your copy before you wait)
  is in `block_eq`. The two points v1 missed were missed by every run, so they were the skill's and the
  guide's to give; v2 gives them, and both v2 runs took them.
- **The skill's clear effect is cost**: v2 against MCP, Opus -55% turns, -69% tokens in, -50% out;
  Sonnet -56% turns, -73% tokens in, -52% out (the MCP arms include the resume).

## Changes made after the runs

- `waveflow-pipeline` rule 5: the token carries the job's id and the stage that takes it checks it
  against its own copy of the command; a mismatch is an error.
- Rule 6: state which (drain or deep link), why, **and what each costs** (a buffer of the largest job in
  the stage, or a FIFO of that many words on the link). The shape's comment and the self-check say
  the same.
