## Findings and what was done

| finding | done |
|---|---|

| `wf_cint.h` written by no step; an integer `ComplexField`'s lane routines include it | **fixed**: `ArrayUtilsStep` writes it beside the header; `tests/hw/test_arrayutils.py` |
