{%- comment -%} Math rendering. just-the-docs ships no math renderer, so the LaTeX in the guide and example pages ($...$ inline, $$...$$ display) is typeset client-side by MathJax 3, loaded from a CDN. kramdown (GFM input) turns $$...$$ into \[...\] / \(...\); single-dollar inline math passes through as text and is picked up here. processEscapes lets a page write \$ for a literal dollar. {%- endcomment -%}
