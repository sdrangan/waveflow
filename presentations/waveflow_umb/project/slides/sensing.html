Our work · Sensing and robotics

Locating a transmitter from one RF snapshot

The platform

Two-channel Pi-Radio FR3 SDR at 10 GHz on a mobile robot

The output

A joint posterior over position and heading, not a point estimate

The data

Real-to-sim calibrated ray tracing, plus robot measurements

MAGNETAR: multipath-guided spatial posteriors for transmitter pose inference in the upper mid-band · arXiv:2609.20670

Directional FR3 antennas mean a receiver sees both where a transmitter is and which way it faces; one snapshot is consistent with several poses, so the method outputs a posterior a robot can act on and fuse over time. Trained mostly on a real-to-sim calibrated simulator with 1,800 measured records; evaluated on 24,000 held-out measurements.
