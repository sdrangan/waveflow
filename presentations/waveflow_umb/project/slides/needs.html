What next-generation radios need

Spectral agility

  • Frequency hopping for macro-diversity
  • Sensing for co-existence
  • Joint sensing and communications

Scale

  • Beamforming gain
  • Interference and satellite nulling
  • Wideband arrays, 1000+ elements

Together: a reconfigurable digital machine behind 1000+ antennas. Nobody yet knows how to build that efficiently.

This is the hand-off from systems to hardware. Both needs push toward a fully digital array whose processing changes at runtime: agility means the dataflow changes; scale means the machine is enormous.
