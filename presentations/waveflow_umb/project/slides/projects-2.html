Project ideas, 2 of 2 · each question is general; Waveflow is the test vehicle

These follow the three open problems on the agents slide. The boundaries: encapsulation is how the work is divided, exploration is which designs to try, verification is how to trust the result. 4: baselines are one agent with the whole codebase, or several agents without typed interfaces. 5: the harder question is architectural moves, such as moving a tasklet or re-partitioning across tiles; use a design space small enough to evaluate exhaustively as ground truth. A timed simulator within 3 to 4 times an untimed model is the prerequisite. 6: agents verify RTL with seeded bugs using an end-to-end golden, a hand-aligned model, or Waveflow; end-to-end metrics hide bugs behind saturation, averaging and decoding. The contribution is about structure, not a gap in today's models, so it should not age as models improve. This is also the Qualcomm lesson: systems models partitioned like the ASIC.
