Project ideas · Applications driving the architecture

What the hardware has to do

1

Distributed massive MIMO

How should processing be split across tiles, and what does each split cost?

Measure: inter-tile bandwidth and power vs. SINR loss; chain vs. hub

2

Integrated sensing and communication

Which processing elements can sensing and communication share on a reconfigurable chip?

Measure: throughput lost per sensing update rate; area of sensing-only blocks

3

Resilience and spectrum sharing

What structures let a radio detect interference and react fast: null, hop, re-partition?

Measure: time to mitigate; capacity under interference; compute held in reserve

Project ideas, 1 of 2

Each is a broad area for a 6 to 12 month study, with a paper as the first deliverable. 1: follows from the budget, where interconnect dominates; the low-rank long-term projection is itself a way to compress inter-tile traffic. 2: on the tasklet machine, sensing is another task stream sharing PEs: FFTs for OFDM and range-Doppler, systolic arrays for beamforming in both; detection and tracking are sensing-only. 3: sensing in service of the link rather than as a service; builds on our satellite nulling, anti-jamming and learned-denoiser work, and gives microsecond reconfiguration a reason to exist. Method for all three: tasklet mapping and scheduling, and PE design (queues, tasklet slots, packet granularity).
