Our work · Satellite co-existence

Sharing with satellites by nulling toward them

Terrestrial base stations place nulls toward line-of-sight satellites while keeping the main beam.

The same idea gives multi-satellite anti-jamming against ground jammers.

Both need a large number of antennas.

Kang et al., ICC 2024 · Jia et al., Globecom 2025

Co-existence is the regulatory gate for FR3. Our results: interference nulling for terrestrial-satellite sharing, and distributed anti-jamming with significant capacity gains. Takeaway for the architecture: antenna count is driven by co-existence, not only by capacity.
